# Customer Refund and Returns Policy

## Page 1

Customer Refund and Returns Policy - General Terms
Refunds can be requested within 30 days of product delivery.
Proof of purchase, order receipt, or invoice number is mandatory for all claims.
Returned items must be undamaged, unworn, and in their original packaging with intact tags.
Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Page 2

Exclusions, Digital Goods, and Return Shipping Fees
Digital downloads, software licenses, personalized products, and perishable goods are strictly non-refundable.
A standard restocking and return shipping fee of $5.99 applies to all elective returns.
Return shipping fees are waived if the item arrived damaged or defective from our warehouse.
Claims for defective items must be submitted within 48 hours of initial delivery.
