# Subscription Plans and Cancellation Terms

## Page 1

Subscription Plans and Billing Renewals
Recurring subscriptions renew automatically at the conclusion of each monthly or annual billing cycle.
Customers have a 14-day cooling-off window following an annual renewal to request a full refund.
Cancellations take effect at the conclusion of the currently paid billing period.
Customers maintain uninterrupted access to premium services until the expiration date.

## Page 2

Mid-Term Cancellations and Enterprise Agreements
Monthly consumer subscription plans do not offer pro-rated refunds for mid-month cancellations.
Annual enterprise contracts may request pro-rated cancellation upon providing 30 days written notice.
Early contract termination fees may apply to enterprise customized tiers as specified in individual master service agreements.
Downgrading from an annual to a monthly plan takes effect at the next renewal cycle.
