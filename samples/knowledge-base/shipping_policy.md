# Shipping and Delivery Guidelines

## Page 1

Shipping and Delivery Guidelines - Shipping Options
Standard domestic shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is complimentary on orders over $50.
Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days for a flat surcharge of $14.99.
Orders submitted before 2 PM Eastern Time are dispatched on the same business day.
Orders placed over weekends or statutory holidays are fulfilled on the next business day.

## Page 2

International Shipments and Lost Parcel Inquiries
International shipping typically takes 7 to 14 business days subject to customs clearance.
Customs duties and import taxes are the sole responsibility of the recipient.
If a parcel tracking status is stalled, lost parcel claims must be filed within 14 days of the estimated delivery date.
Approved lost parcel claims are eligible for an immediate free reshipment or store credit.
